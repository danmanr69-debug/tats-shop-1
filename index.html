The Dragonfly story
“Resilience. Growth. Keep going.”

Dragonfly Tattoo Co. was opened by Kari Chapman in downtown Adrian in October 2024 — a longtime dream turned into a personal, artist-led studio.

The dragonfly reflects resilience: even when damaged, it keeps moving forward. That idea sits at the heart of the studio — creating art that feels personal, lasting, and completely yours.
